Insurance Navigator SOP Knowledge Base (2025–26)
Wellness at Penn — Public Health and Wellbeing — Student Health & Counseling
1) Executive Summary (for leadership)
What this workflow is: A consolidated, plain-language Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Insurance Navigators who support students with the University-sponsored health insurance (PSIP), waiver reviews, referrals, alternatives counseling, supplemental plans, and related communications across phone, email, and in-person channels. Source of truth is limited to the uploaded Playbook, training deck, and the completed NavigAItor Workflow Questionnaire. 
Why consistency matters:
•	Operational risk — incorrect guidance can cause default enrollment, missed windows (e.g., 7/1–8/31), referral penalties, or out-of-network billing exposure.
•	Equity implications — uneven explanations of waiver criteria, HMOs, or ISO/PSI exclusions can advantage students with prior insurance literacy and disadvantage others.
•	Student experience impact — inconsistent answers across channels (phone, inboxes) erode trust; clear scripts, approved language, and predictable routing improve resolution time.
What this SOP enables staff to do reliably:
•	Guide students through enroll/waive using the Borchardt portal and document outcomes.
•	Explain PSIP benefits and the Clinical Fee, including referral rules and exceptions.
•	Identify when alternatives (Pennie, ISO/PSI) may meet waiver criteria and clearly warn about exclusions and SHS out-of-network implications.
•	Execute phone/email workflows with identity verification, minimum necessary information, and compliant language.
•	Apply Stop & Route triggers and escalate unclear, high-risk, or ambiguous cases without guessing.
2) Quickstart (for new staff)
“If you only read one page, read this.”
5 most common scenarios → what to do
1.	Enroll/Waive during 7/1–8/31: Send student to studenthealth.bgenroll.com (PennKey). If unsure about waiver eligibility, advise to select manual verification and upload details/screenshots. Document outcomes in portal notes. 
2.	Waiver check (Five Gates): Verify U.S. insurer, $2,000,000 annual max, pre-existing covered, Philadelphia medical access, Philadelphia mental health access. Out-of-area HMOs need Philly network or guest membership; emergency-only coverage fails.
3.	Referrals on PSIP near campus: Most Philly-area specialty care requires an SHS referral. Exceptions include emergencies, OB/GYN, pediatrics, MH/SUD, annual eye exam & select vision services, dental injury, impacted wisdom teeth, >25 miles, or SHS closed. Route to SHS Referral Coordinator for issuance/renewal.
4.	Missed 8/31 or loses coverage later: Default PSIP enrollment occurs after 8/31. If loss of other coverage occurs after the window, use QLE to enroll within 31 days; for spring waivers after gaining other qualifying coverage, use LCW Jan 1–31.
5.	ISO/PSI or Pennie questions: Explain that SHS is out-of-network for private plans; routine SHS visits are $0 with the Clinical Fee, but labs/meds/procedures bill to insurance. Warn explicitly about common ISO/PSI exclusions and possible out-of-pocket risk.
Explicit Stop & Route triggers (bullet list)
•	Safety concerns or crisis language → Stop & warm handoff per office protocol (owner: Supervisor/SHS leadership).
•	Billing disputes beyond scope or complex claim denials → Route to Student Health Billing.
•	Unclear waiver eligibility (ambiguous network/MH access, Medicaid outside PA/South Jersey, complex HMO riders) → Escalate to Supervisor for review.
•	Referral edge cases (distance, closed hours, non-listed services) → Route to SHS Referral Coordinator.
•	Any request to approve exceptions, remove holds, or alter charges → Supervisor/Policy Owner only.
What staff should never decide on their own
•	Granting or denying waivers outside the documented Five Gates.
•	Approving exceptions to referral requirements.
•	Issuing refunds, removing financial holds, or changing charges.
•	Interpreting unclear plan documents without escalation.
•	Providing legal or immigration advice.

4.7 QLEs (Enroll after loss) & LCWs (Spring waiver after gain)

Scope

•	Out of scope: Accepting non-qualifying reasons.
Inputs staff need (minimum)
•	For QLE: proof of loss with dates; for LCW: proof of new active coverage with start date; completed applications.
Step-by-step process — QLE (Enroll)
49.	Student contacts office; send QLE application.
50.	Student returns signed application with dated proof of loss to wel-shsinsur@pobox.upenn.edu within 31 days.
51.	Effective date typically day after prior coverage ends (Aetna confirms).

•	Age-26 dependent aging out; divorce; employer loss; Medicaid loss — handle via QLE within 31 days.

5) Standard Definitions (Plain Language)
•	Premium: the membership fee you pay to have the plan; due even if you don’t use care.
•	Deductible: the amount you pay first each plan year before the plan starts sharing costs.
•	Copay: a flat dollar fee for a visit or service (e.g., $35 for urgent care).
•	Coinsurance: a percentage of the cost you pay after meeting the deductible (e.g., 20%).
•	Out-of-Pocket Maximum: the cap on your spending for covered in-network care each year; after reaching it, the plan pays 100% of covered costs.
•	Clinical Fee: a mandatory fee that covers routine SHS visits at $0; not insurance.
•	Referral (PSIP): an authorization from SHS needed for most Philadelphia-area specialty care on PSIP (see exceptions).
